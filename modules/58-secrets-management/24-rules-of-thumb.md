## 📏 Rules of Thumb

### The Secrets Rules

| Rule | Description | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Never commit secrets** | No hardcoded credentials | Security |
| **Use a vault** | Centralized secret storage | Control |
| **Rotate regularly** | Limit exposure window | Compliance |
| **Audit access** | Know who accessed what | Accountability |

---

**Why these rules matters:** Following these rules ensures secure secrets management.
