**Part 19: Software Repositories and PPAs — Package Sources**

You will learn:
- Understanding repository architecture
- Debian/Ubuntu repositories (sources.list, PPAs)
- RPM repositories (yum.repos.d, EPEL, RPM Fusion)

### Why This Matters
Repositories are where your packages come from. Understanding how they work lets you add software sources confidently, avoid dependency conflicts, and keep your systems up to date with the right packages.
