## 📏 Rules of Thumb

### The Observability Rules

| Rule | Description | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Instrument everything** | You can't fix what you can't see | Visibility |
| **Alert on symptoms** | Not causes | Actionable |
| **Use structured logs** | JSON format | Searchable |
| **Set SLOs** | Define reliability targets | Business alignment |

---

**Why these rules matters:** Following these rules ensures effective system observability.
