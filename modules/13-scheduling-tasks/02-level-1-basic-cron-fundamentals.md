> **Level 1 Goal:** Understand how the cron daemon works, master the 5-field crontab syntax, and know where different types of crontabs live on the system.

### What You'll Cover
- The cron daemon and how it processes jobs
- The 5-field crontab syntax (minute, hour, day, month, weekday)
- User crontabs vs system crontabs (`/etc/cron.d/`)
- Cron directories: `/etc/cron.daily/`, `/etc/cron.weekly/`
