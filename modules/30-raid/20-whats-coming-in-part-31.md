### How Part 30 Connects
LVM sits on top of RAID arrays, adding another layer of flexibility. While RAID protects against drive failures, LVM lets you create, resize, and snapshot volumes without touching the underlying RAID configuration. Together, they form the foundation of enterprise storage management.
