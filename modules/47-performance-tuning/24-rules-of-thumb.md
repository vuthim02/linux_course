## 📏 Rules of Thumb

### The Performance Tuning Rules

| Rule | Description | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Measure first** | Don't guess | Evidence |
| **Tune one thing** | Isolate changes | Scientific |
| **Benchmark before/after** | Prove improvement | Validation |
| **Document changes** | Know what you did | Maintainability |

---

**Why these rules matters:** Following these rules ensures effective performance tuning.
