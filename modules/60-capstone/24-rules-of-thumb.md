## 📏 Rules of Thumb

### The Capstone Rules

| Rule | Description | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Start small** | Build incrementally | Manageable |
| **Test early** | Catch issues early | Quality |
| **Document as you go** | Don't leave it for last | Completeness |
| **Present clearly** | Explain what you built | Communication |

---

**Why these rules matters:** Following these rules ensures successful capstone completion.
