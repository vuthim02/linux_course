> **Level 2 Goal:** Add and manage PPAs on Ubuntu, configure RPM repositories on Fedora/RHEL, and enable EPEL and RPM Fusion for additional packages.

### What You'll Cover
- Adding and removing PPAs with `add-apt-repository`
- RPM repository structure in `/etc/yum.repos.d/`
- Configuring `.repo` files: baseurl, gpgcheck, enabled
- Enabling EPEL on RHEL/CentOS/Fedora
- RPM Fusion: free and nonfree repositories for Fedora
