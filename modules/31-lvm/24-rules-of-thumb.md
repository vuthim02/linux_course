## 📏 Rules of Thumb

### The LVM Rules

| Rule | Description | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Use LVM for flexibility** | Resize online | Adaptability |
| **Plan VG capacity** | Leave room for growth | Future-proofing |
| **Use snapshots for backups** | Consistent state | Data integrity |
| **Monitor free space** | VG can fill up | Prevention |

---

**Why these rules matters:** Following these rules ensures flexible and reliable storage management.
