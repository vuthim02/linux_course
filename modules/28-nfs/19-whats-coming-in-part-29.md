### How Part 28 Connects
NFS and Samba serve the same purpose — sharing files over the network — but for different ecosystems. NFS dominates Unix/Linux; Samba bridges Linux and Windows. Understanding both lets you build heterogeneous environments where Linux, Windows, and macOS clients all access the same data.
