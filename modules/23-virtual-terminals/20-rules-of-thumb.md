## 📏 Rules of Thumb

### The TTY Rules

| Rule | Description | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Keep a TTY open** | Emergency access | Safety net |
| **Use tmux/screen** | Persistent sessions | Don't lose work |
| **Alt+F7 for GUI** | When X11 crashes | Recovery |
| **Check tty** | Know where you are | Debugging |

---

**Why these rules matter:** Virtual terminals are your safety net when graphical interfaces fail.
