## 📏 Rules of Thumb

### The Immutable Rules

| Rule | Description | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Never SSH into containers** | They're disposable | Repeatability |
| **Use version tags** | Not just latest | Traceability |
| **Keep images small** | Less attack surface | Security |
| **Test in CI** | Before deploy | Quality |

---

**Why these rules matters:** Following these rules ensures reliable immutable deployments.
