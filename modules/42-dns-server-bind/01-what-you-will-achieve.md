### Why This Part Matters
Running your own DNS server gives you complete control over name resolution — critical for internal services, split-horizon DNS, and environments where third-party DNS is not an option. BIND is the most widely deployed DNS server software in the world, and understanding it is fundamental to network administration.

> **Real-world perspective**: Every internal service, database connection, and API call depends on DNS. When your DNS server is slow or misconfigured, everything slows down. Running BIND with proper redundancy and DNSSEC ensures your infrastructure's foundation is solid.
