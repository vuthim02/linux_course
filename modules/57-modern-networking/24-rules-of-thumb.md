## 📏 Rules of Thumb

### The Network Rules

| Rule | Description | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Document your subnets** | Know your network | Troubleshooting |
| **Use DNS names** | Not IPs | Flexibility |
| **Monitor bandwidth** | Catch bottlenecks | Performance |
| **Test connectivity** | Before debugging | Efficiency |

---

**Why these rules matters:** Following these rules ensures reliable network operations.
