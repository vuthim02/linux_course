## 📏 Rules of Thumb

### The CI/CD Rules

| Rule | Description | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Commit frequently** | Small changes | Easier debugging |
| **Automate tests** | Catch regressions | Quality |
| **Deploy often** | Reduce risk | Confidence |
| **Rollback fast** | Fix issues quickly | Reliability |

---

**Why these rules matters:** Following these rules ensures smooth software delivery.
