## 📏 Rules of Thumb

### The Container Rules

| Rule | Description | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **One process per container** | Keep focused | Maintainability |
| **Use specific tags** | Not `latest` | Reproducibility |
| **Minimize layers** | Smaller images | Efficiency |
| **Run as non-root** | Security | Least privilege |
| **Use health checks** | Reliability | Self-healing |

---

**Why these rules matters:** Following these rules ensures your containers are secure and efficient.
