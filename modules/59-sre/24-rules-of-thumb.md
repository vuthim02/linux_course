## 📏 Rules of Thumb

### The SRE Rules

| Rule | Description | Why |
|------|-------------|-----|
| **Automate toil** | Stop manual work | Efficiency |
| **Set error budgets** | Balance speed/reliability | Trade-offs |
| **Learn from incidents** | Postmortems improve systems | Growth |
| **Monitor everything** | You can't fix what you can't see | Visibility |

---

**Why these rules matters:** Following these rules ensures reliable system operations.
