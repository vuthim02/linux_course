## 📚 What's Coming in Part 33

**Part 33: System Monitoring**

You will learn:
- Why monitoring matters and what to monitor (the USE method)
- `/proc` interfaces: `/proc/stat`, `/proc/meminfo`, `/proc/loadavg`, `/proc/diskstats`
- Interactive monitoring: `top`, `htop`, `free`, `ps`, `ss`
- Advanced diagnostics: `vmstat`, `iostat`, `mpstat`, `dstat`, `nmon`, `glances`
- Log-based monitoring: journald, logwatch, auditd
- Web-based administration: Cockpit
- Prometheus + node_exporter for metrics collection and alerting
- S.M.A.R.T. disk health monitoring and predictive failure
- System diagnostic reporting with `sosreport`
- 15 hands-on practices

### How Part 32 Connects
Monitoring detects the failures that trigger your backup恢复 procedures. You learned how to back up data; now you'll learn how to detect problems before they destroy it. Together, backup and monitoring form the two pillars of data protection.
